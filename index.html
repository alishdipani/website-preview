Currently, I'm turning toward AI safety.

I'm a NeuroAI PhD student at Georgia Tech, advised by Apurva Ratan Murty and Dobromir Rahnev, and supported by the CoCo Fellowship from the Center of Excellence in Computational Cognition. My background is in computer science, vision science, and computational cognitive neuroscience. Previously, I was a research assistant at MIT Brain and Cognitive Sciences, Northeastern University, and TCS Innovation Labs, and a machine learning engineer at Baylor College of Medicine. Beyond research, I do open-source development and content creation, mainly through Neuromatch Academy, where I develop teaching material for its courses in computational neuroscience, deep learning, and NeuroAI.
