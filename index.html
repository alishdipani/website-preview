Neuro & cognitive science for AI

Probing AI systems with controlled experiments from human cognition and psychophysics, and with tools from cognitive neuroscience.

AI for neuro & cognitive science

Building AI models that predict human brain activity and behavior, and agents that conduct in-silico experiments.

Lately, I have started to grow more concerned about AI safety and have started learning about it. (why?)

I'm a NeuroAI PhD student at Georgia Tech, advised by Apurva Ratan Murty and Dobromir Rahnev, and supported by the CoCo Fellowship from the Center of Excellence in Computational Cognition. My background is in computer science, vision science, and computational cognitive neuroscience. Previously, I was a research assistant at MIT Brain and Cognitive Sciences, Northeastern University, and TCS Innovation Labs, and a machine learning engineer at Baylor College of Medicine. Beyond research, I do open-source development and content creation, mainly through Neuromatch Academy.
